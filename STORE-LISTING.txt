NAME:
Swipe for Letterboxd

SUMMARY (≤132 chars):
A swipe-style deck plus a local database to triage, filter, and search your own Letterboxd watch history — fast.

DESCRIPTION:
Swipe for Letterboxd adds a Tinder-style swipe deck and a private, on-device film
database to Letterboxd, so you can move through films quickly and make sense of
your own viewing history.

WHAT IT DOES
• Swipe deck — On listing pages (popular, watchlist, lists), open a deck of FRESH
  films (ones you haven't watched, liked, watch-listed, or skipped) and act on each
  with a visible button, swipe, or keyboard shortcut: watched, like, watchlist,
  or skip.
• Local profile database — Run Collection Sync (while logged in) to build an
  on-device registry of your watched films with title, year, director, genres,
  poster, star rating, liked status, and review text where Letterboxd exposes it.
• Filter, sort, search — Search your history and filter by watched / liked /
  watchlist / rated / reviewed / genre / watched-date, then sort by rating, year,
  watched date, or title. Open any film for a detail view.
• Trailers — Press T or use the Trailer control to open the current film's
  Letterboxd trailer page without losing your place in the deck. On that page,
  press K to start the unopened YouTube trailer, then K or Space to pause/resume.
• Quick review — Write and submit a star rating + review from the deck, with
  browser dictation where the browser supplies a working recognition service.
  Swipe prevents a known existing diary entry from being mistaken for an edit;
  logging another viewing requires an explicit Rewatch confirmation.
• Toolbar status and actions — Check whether the current Letterboxd tab is ready,
  see pending account actions, resume Swipe, start profile sync, or open settings
  from the extension popup.

PRIVACY & WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
• Your film registry is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage). There is no
  account with us or developer-operated server, and nothing is sent to us.
• Your Letterboxd username and a matching local account ID are stored in the browser
  only to keep different profiles separated. They are never sent to the developer.
• Preferences sync via Chrome's built-in sync storage only.
• Dictation uses the browser or operating system speech service; the extension does
  not receive or store microphone audio. Trailer links may load Letterboxd and its
  YouTube player only after the user opens one.
• On an explicit Review Submit, the review/rating/date/tags/options and a transient
  CSRF token are sent directly to Letterboxd over HTTPS. They are never sent to the
  extension developer, an analytics service, or a developer-operated server.
• Clearing local film data keeps Chrome-synced interface preferences. If an account
  action or review may already have reached Letterboxd, Swipe retains only a minimal
  account/film verification record until the film is checked. Unsent actions and
  drafts are removed; the retained records contain no review text, submitted review
  payload, cookie, or CSRF token.

DATA COLLECTION:
• Personally identifiable information: YES — the detected Letterboxd username and
  matching local account ID are stored ON YOUR DEVICE to keep profile data separated.
  They are never sent to the developer or any third party.
• Authentication information: YES — the active Letterboxd session and CSRF token are
  used transiently only to authenticate an account action the user explicitly requests.
  Swipe does not store the token or cookies, and sends them only to Letterboxd over HTTPS.
• Web history: YES — Swipe handles the current Letterboxd URL and film/profile/list URLs
  needed for its deck, trailer, sync, and database features. It does not record or inspect
  browsing outside the declared Letterboxd pages or send this history to the developer.
• User activity: YES — explicit Swipe choices such as watched, like, watchlist, skip,
  and review are used to update the local registry and, where requested, Letterboxd.
  It does not log unrelated clicks, pointer movement, scrolling, or keystrokes.
• Website content: YES — the extension reads your own Letterboxd profile pages
  (film titles, ratings, liked status, URLs, and review text) to build the local database,
  and handles review form text, tags, date, rating, and options when you submit them.
  Synced profile content is stored ON YOUR DEVICE; submitted review data is sent directly
  to Letterboxd only. None is transmitted to the extension developer or analytics.
• Health / Financial / Location / Personal communications: No

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE (also stated in the README)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Collection Sync makes a number of authenticated requests to letterboxd.com AS YOU
  (your logged-in session) to read your watched films, watchlist, and likes. Requests
  are throttled and back off on rate-limit responses.
• Marking watched/liked/watchlist and submitting reviews WRITE to your real Letterboxd
  account using your active session. Review submission posts to a Letterboxd endpoint
  that is not a public, documented API and may change without notice. On explicit Submit,
  the review, rating, diary date, rewatch/spoiler/like options, tags, and transient CSRF
  token go directly to Letterboxd over HTTPS.
• Dictation availability and audio processing depend on the browser's speech service.
  Swipe does not add a microphone extension permission or receive the audio itself.
• This is an unofficial, independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by
  Letterboxd. Use it in accordance with Letterboxd's Terms of Use.

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

storage:
Required to store the user's device-local film registry, collection-sync metadata,
account-link status, deck filters, and extension preferences in chrome.storage.
This data stays in the user's browser and is not sent to the developer.

unlimitedStorage:
Required because large Letterboxd histories can contain thousands of local film
records, including poster URLs, ratings, collection flags, watched dates, and review
text. It prevents the normal extension storage quota from truncating a user's own
local database. It does not grant access to arbitrary files on the device.

Host permission — https://letterboxd.com/*:
Required to detect supported Letterboxd film and listing pages, read the signed-in
user's own collection pages during an explicit sync, open Letterboxd trailer controls,
and perform watched, liked, or watchlist actions the user explicitly chooses. Requests
remain limited to Letterboxd and use the user's active session.

Host permission — https://api.letterboxd.com/*:
Required only for the extension service worker to submit a review or rating the user
explicitly confirms. The fixed request sends the review/rating/date/tags/options and a
transient CSRF token directly to Letterboxd over HTTPS. No arbitrary URL is accepted,
and no data is sent to the developer or a developer-operated service.

LIMITED USE DISCLOSURE:
Swipe uses the Letterboxd account, page, form, and activity data it handles only to
provide and improve the user-facing deck, review, trailer, sync, and local-database
features described above. It does not sell this data, use it for advertising or
creditworthiness, transfer it for unrelated purposes, or give the developer or
other people access to it for unrelated processing. Content that the user explicitly
submits to Letterboxd is handled according to their Letterboxd account and privacy
settings.
