# v6.3.0-beta.6 review-state recovery specification

## Problem

After **Clear local film data**, opening Review and submitting a new review could remain blocked until Letterboxd was refreshed. Clearing removed the active local account state, while the still-open Letterboxd page already knew the signed-in username.

## Required behaviour

- Saving a valid review draft from the current signed-in Letterboxd page may reactivate that account when local state is absent or contains only an empty legacy account created by the clear operation.
- The review can be submitted immediately without refreshing the Letterboxd page.
- Reactivation must never replace a different active Letterboxd account.
- Invalid or oversized review-draft records must not mutate storage.
- Review drafts remain isolated by account and film and stay subject to the documented retention limit.
- Clearing local film data retains its documented privacy behaviour and must not restore stale film records, drafts, sync metadata, or queued work.

## Regression expectations

- Review submission still uses the fixed Letterboxd production endpoint and a transient CSRF token.
- Trailer shortcuts, dictation fallback, skip behaviour, Undo, automatic pagination, account switching, and package reproducibility retain their beta.5 behaviour.
- The visible release identifier is `v6.3.0-beta.6` everywhere it is user-facing or package-critical.
